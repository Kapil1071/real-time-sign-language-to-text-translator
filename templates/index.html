The End-to-End Processing Pipeline

How raw photons from your webcam transform into synthesized audible language in milliseconds.

STAGE 01 📷

Optical Stream Ingestion

Captures continuous 60Hz webcam video frames, applying horizontal mirroring and RGB color-space conversion for natural user feedback.

STAGE 02 🖐️

21-Joint Skeletal Mapping

MediaPipe Hands detects and maps 21 distinct 3D joint coordinates across the palm and fingers, providing sub-millisecond precision.

STAGE 03 📐

Dynamic Region of Interest

Derives an adaptive bounding box around the active hand geometry with padding, completely stripping away irrelevant background clutter.

STAGE 04 🧠

Deep Convolutional CNN

Resizes the isolated hand crop to 64×64 RGB, passing it through 3 Conv2D + MaxPooling layers with Dropout regularization and Softmax output.

STAGE 05 ⏱️

Temporal Stability Debounce

Requires consistent predictions across consecutive frames (≥70% confidence) to prevent unintended flickers and micro-tremors from entering text.

STAGE 06 🔊

Offline Voice Synthesis

Assembles finalized characters into coherent sentences with gesture-based space and delete controls, vocalizing speech via native OS engines.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sign2Text operate without an internet connection? ▼
The entire system is hosted locally on your device via Flask. MediaPipe models, the TensorFlow CNN classifier (model.h5), and the pyttsx3 speech engine all reside in local memory, requiring zero external API requests.
Why is MediaPipe combined with a Convolutional Neural Network? ▼
Raw webcam frames contain substantial visual noise like clothes, walls, and furniture. MediaPipe locates the 21 geometric joint landmarks of the hand, which allows us to tightly crop just the hand pixels before feeding it to the CNN, dramatically improving classification accuracy.
What is the purpose of the temporal stability buffer? ▼
In real-world webcam conditions, hand micro-tremors and natural lighting changes can cause momentary single-frame mispredictions. The stability buffer requires the same sign to be predicted consistently for 12 consecutive frames (at ≥70% confidence) before appending it to the sentence.
Can the system be expanded to new signs or numbers? ▼
Yes. Using the included `collect_data.py` script, you can capture custom image folders for numbers (0-9) or specialized vocabulary, and re-train in under two minutes with `python train_model.py`.
